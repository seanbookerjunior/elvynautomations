A 2 minute walkthrough of what happens when a customer calls and you can't pick up.

Where the money leaks

Most home service businesses don't have a lead problem. They have a follow-up problem. These are the spots where jobs fall through.

After-hours calls

Someone with an emergency isn't leaving a voicemail. If you don't pick up, they call the next number.

Quotes that go quiet

You drove out, looked at the job, and sent a number. Then nothing, because nobody followed up.

No-shows

The appointment was booked, nobody reminded them, and your tech showed up to an empty house.

Past customers

Everyone you've ever worked for is a repeat job waiting to happen. Most of them never hear from you again.

Reviews

Happy customers don't leave reviews unless you ask at the right time. Fewer reviews means fewer calls next month.

What we install

One system that follows every lead from the first call to the paid invoice. It runs on your business number and your calendar. Your part is a couple of taps on your phone.

  1. Missed call text back

    Every missed call gets a text within a minute, day or night, so the customer talks to you instead of calling someone else.

  2. Instant lead response

    Form fills and ad leads get an immediate reply that asks the right questions: what's wrong, where, how urgent.

  3. Booking and reminders

    Leads can book into times you choose, or you schedule them yourself. Either way, reminder texts go out before every appointment to cut no-shows.

  4. Estimate follow-up

    Tap once to mark an estimate as sent. If it isn't accepted, the system follows up for you over the next two weeks until you get a yes or a no.

  5. Review requests

    When you mark a job done, the customer gets a text asking for a Google review while the work is fresh.

  6. Past customer reactivation

    Your old customer list gets seasonal check-ins: seasonal tune-ups, maintenance reminders, winter prep. Repeat work without cold calling.

Questions

Do I have to learn new software?

No. We build and run it. You get texts and bookings on your phone like you do now, just more of them. Your only part is tapping once when you send an estimate or finish a job.

Will my customers know it's automated?

The messages are written in your voice and sent from a local number tied to your business. You approve every message before it goes live.

Can I control when customers book?

Yes. Customers can book themselves into times you choose, send requests you approve before anything hits your calendar, or you can schedule everyone yourself. The system just makes sure every lead gets a reply right away so they don't call someone else while you're busy.

What if I already have a CRM or booking tool?

Tell us what you use on the audit call. In most cases we connect to what you have or replace the parts that aren't doing anything for you.

What does it cost?

It depends on your call volume and service area. We go over pricing on the audit call once we know what your business actually needs.
